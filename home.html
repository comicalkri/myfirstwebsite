my family and i

My parents and I are super close. They are also my biggest supporters, and i really would not know what I would do without them.

My extended family and I are also very close. We try to get together every year when time allows it.

I love learning, so I loved going to school. There were many times I had let my grades define me, but I know now to have a balance between school and personal life.

me as a school girl (through the years!)

I went to Sanctuary Christian Academy for my nursery and kindergarten years. My mom put me in school (speifically nursery) because she realized that I was not interacting with children my age, and she thought enrolling me would force me out of my shell.

In my elementary years, my dad transferred me to a public school (Ozamiz City Central School) because they thought that my old school's small population was not helping me become more confident.

This was also the same time I joined Math and Science quizbees, and this definietly helped boost my confidence because, somehow, I was finally doing something good for myself since sports was never really an option because I had asthma.

In highschool, my parents transferred me to La Salle University - Ozamiz City. This was where I met my closest friends (who are still my friends to this day!).

This is also where I was able to learn and grow to love debating. I was part of the Debate team (and training for major competitions!)when I was in 9th grade, before the pandemic took over. In senior high school, I grew to love the subject Physics, because it was interesting to me.

I grew up knowing what I already wanted to do: to become a nurse and help the community. Somewhere along the way, my dreams have changed: I wanted to become a doctor, a lawyer, a sound enginner, and even a teacher!

where my dream all began!

However, eventually, all roads always lead back to nursing, which meant that all of a sudden, while surviving my 12th grade era, I had a one track mind: come hell or high water, I was going to pursue Nursing.

I actually took a chance, because to be honest, I only applied to 2 schools: MSU-IIT and CMU. If I didn't get a score big enough to help me enter my dream program, I could not imagine what I would have done to myself at that time.
