Anyway, I have always been a big fan of reading, which is really funny because my love for reading was entirely accidental!

When I was younger (around 8 years old), my mom used to force me to read books for Summer Reading Camp. Even though I thought I would find reading boring, I still did it because I was afraid of my mother (LOL!).

Aside from reading, I also enjoy watching different TV shows. My favorite genre is mystery, thriller, and procedural stuff.

My all-time favorite TV show would definitely be Criminal Mind. I think the aura and dynamic of how the TV show is presented is what really got me hooked. In the show, the FBI Behavioral Analysis Unit (BAU) try not to use violence when solving cases, and they use psychology instead.

As someone who has a fascination with psychology, it was no surprise I would enjoy this.

I also really enjoyed High Potential (which has a similar dynamic with Criminal Minds), and The Pitt, which is a show set in a hospital (a medical drama, if you will).


I'm in multiple fandoms, but the one I'm prominently in would definitely be Heated Rivalry, Avengers, and Harry Potter (both the Golden Trio Era and the Marauders Era).

To me, there's something so comforting being in fandoms because you get to share the same love you have for these characters or stories with other people.

Interestingly enough, I've been in multiple fandoms in the past, and Twitter, AO3, and Tumblr used to be my escape from the reality, and although I'm still on these platforms, I can say that I don't use them as much, probably because I don't have the time anymore.
